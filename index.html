Hmm Science

Hmm Science is a short-form science popularization project publishing YouTube Shorts in three languages:

  • Russian — Хмм, наука
  • English — Hmm Science
  • Spanish — Hmm, ciencia

The publishing tool — uses YouTube API Services

We use a private, internal tool to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channels and to read their performance statistics. It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, authorized via Google OAuth by the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other users' data.

By using the tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy.
