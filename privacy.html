Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy describes how the Hmm Science publishing tool (“the App”) handles data. The App is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Hmm Science YouTube channels to manage those channels.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the App accesses

  • OAuth tokens for the channel owner’s Google account, used to authorize API requests.
  • Basic channel information (channel ID and title) to confirm the correct channel was authorized.
  • Metadata and status of videos the App uploads (title, description, privacy status, publish time).
  • Aggregate statistics of those videos from YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, average view percentage, likes, subscribers gained).

How data is used

Data is used only to upload and schedule our own videos and to analyze their performance to improve future content. The App does not access, collect or process data of any other YouTube users or viewers beyond aggregate channel statistics.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and statistics are stored locally on the channel owner’s machine, with access restricted to the owner. Data is not sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties. No cookies or tracking are used by the App or this website.

Retention and deletion

The App stores only data obtained with the channel owner’s authorization, about the owner’s own channels, and only while that authorization is active. The App does not store any unauthorized (public) data of other channels or users. You can revoke the App’s access at any time via Google security settings; after revocation the stored tokens and the API data obtained through them are deleted within 7 days.
